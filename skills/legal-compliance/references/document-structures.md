# Legal Document Structures

## 1. Privacy Policy (`/legal/privacy-policy.md`)

1. **Introduction** — who you are, what this policy covers, effective date
2. **Information We Collect** — list each data type with purpose, legal basis (GDPR), retention period
3. **How We Use Your Information** — purposes mapped to data types and legal bases
4. **How We Share Your Information** — third-party categories, DPA existence, no sale (or sale opt-out if CCPA)
5. **International Data Transfers** — transfer mechanisms (SCCs, adequacy decisions, BCRs)
6. **Data Retention** — per-category retention schedule and deletion process
7. **Your Rights** — jurisdiction-specific rights table (GDPR: access/erasure/portability/rectification/objection; CCPA: know/delete/opt-out/correct/limit; others as applicable)
8. **Cookies and Tracking** — link to Cookie Policy if separate; summary of tracking technologies
9. **Children's Privacy** — minimum age, COPPA compliance if applicable
10. **Security** — technical and organizational measures (without disclosing attack surface)
11. **Changes to This Policy** — how and when users will be notified of changes
12. **Contact Us** — privacy email, DPO contact, mailing address, supervisory authority complaint rights

## 2. Terms of Service (`/legal/terms-of-service.md`)

1. **Acceptance of Terms** — binding agreement on use; age requirement
2. **Account Registration** — eligibility, accurate info obligation, account security responsibility
3. **Acceptable Use** — what users may and may not do; prohibited content/behavior list
4. **Intellectual Property** — who owns what; license granted to users; license granted to you for UGC
5. **Payment Terms** — prices, billing cycles, tax responsibility, payment failure, refund policy
6. **Subscriptions** — auto-renewal disclosure, cancellation process, pro-rated refunds (if any)
7. **Third-Party Services** — disclaimer of responsibility for linked/integrated third-party products
8. **Disclaimer of Warranties** — AS IS, no uptime guarantees, jurisdiction carve-outs
9. **Limitation of Liability** — liability cap (typically fees paid in last 12 months), excluded damages, jurisdiction carve-outs
10. **Indemnification** — user indemnifies you for their violations
11. **Termination** — grounds for suspension/termination; effect on data; survival of clauses
12. **Dispute Resolution** — governing law, jurisdiction, arbitration clause (if US), class action waiver
13. **Changes to Terms** — notice method, continued use = acceptance
14. **Contact Information** — legal contact address

## 3. Cookie Policy (`/legal/cookie-policy.md`)

1. **What Are Cookies** — brief plain-language explanation
2. **Types of Cookies We Use** — table: name / provider / purpose / duration / category (Strictly Necessary / Functional / Analytics / Marketing)
3. **Third-Party Cookies** — list all third-party cookies with links to their opt-out pages
4. **How to Control Cookies** — browser settings, consent banner instructions
5. **Your Choices** — what happens if cookies are disabled (feature degradation)
6. **Cookie Consent (GDPR)** — how consent is obtained, stored, and can be withdrawn
7. **Do Not Track** — whether DNT signals are honored
8. **Changes to This Policy** — update notification method

## 4. Data Processing Agreement (DPA) (`/legal/dpa.md`)

Use when: (a) you act as a **processor** for business customers who control data, OR (b) as a template you require sub-processors to sign.

1. **Definitions** — Controller, Processor, Data Subject, Personal Data (per applicable law)
2. **Subject Matter and Duration** — scope of processing, term tied to main agreement
3. **Nature and Purpose of Processing** — the processing activities covered
4. **Types of Personal Data** — list data categories processed
5. **Categories of Data Subjects** — whose data (customers, employees, end-users, etc.)
6. **Obligations of the Processor** — process only on documented instructions; confidentiality; security measures; sub-processor rules; data subject rights assistance; breach notification; deletion/return on termination; audit cooperation
7. **Sub-Processors** — list of approved sub-processors (Annex); mechanism to object to new ones
8. **International Transfers** — transfer mechanism (SCCs Annex if needed)
9. **Security Measures** (Annex) — technical and organizational measures (TOMs)
10. **Contact and Governing Law**

## 5. Records of Processing Activities (RoPA) (`/legal/ropa.md`)

*Required under GDPR Article 30 for controllers with 250+ employees or processing that is not occasional, involves special categories, or is likely to affect data subjects' rights.*

Table columns for each processing activity:

| Column | Description |
|---|---|
| Activity Name | Descriptive label (e.g., "User Account Management") |
| Controller | Your legal entity name and contact |
| DPO | Name and contact of Data Protection Officer |
| Purpose | Specific purpose(s) for processing |
| Legal Basis | GDPR Article 6 basis (+ Art 9 for special categories) |
| Data Categories | Types of personal data processed |
| Data Subject Categories | Whose data (customers, employees, prospects…) |
| Recipients | Internal departments + third-party recipients/processors |
| Third Countries | Any transfers outside EEA + transfer mechanism |
| Retention | How long data is kept + deletion trigger |
| Security Measures | Reference to TOMs document or brief description |
