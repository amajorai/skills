# Regulation Applicability Matrix

## GDPR (EU/EEA)
**Applies if:** users in the EU/EEA, OR company established in EU/EEA, OR offering goods/services to EU residents, OR monitoring EU residents' behavior.  
**Key requirements:** lawful basis for processing, data subject rights (access, erasure, portability, rectification, objection), DPA with processors, breach notification within 72 hours, privacy by design, DPO if large-scale processing of sensitive data.

## UK GDPR
**Applies if:** users in the UK post-Brexit.  
Nearly identical to EU GDPR but a separate regime. Requires UK representative if no UK establishment.

## CCPA/CPRA (California, USA)
**Applies if:** for-profit business with California residents as users AND meets ONE of: >$25M annual revenue, OR buys/sells/shares data of 100,000+ consumers/households/devices, OR derives 50%+ revenue from selling/sharing personal data.  
Note: even below thresholds, including CCPA rights is best practice.  
**Key requirements:** right to know, right to delete, right to opt-out of sale/sharing, right to correct, right to limit sensitive PI, non-discrimination.

## PIPEDA (Canada)
**Applies if:** commercial activity involving personal information of Canadian residents.  
**Key requirements:** consent, purpose limitation, individual access rights, breach notification to Privacy Commissioner and affected individuals.

## LGPD (Brazil)
**Applies if:** processing data of individuals in Brazil, processing carried out in Brazil, or offering goods/services to individuals in Brazil.  
**Key requirements:** lawful basis, data subject rights, DPO (encarregado), breach notification within 2 business days.

## PDPA (Singapore, 2012 amended 2020)
**Applies if:** processing personal data of individuals in Singapore for commercial purposes (extraterritorial reach).  
**Key requirements:** purpose limitation, consent (or legitimate interests in B2B), access/correction rights, breach notification to PDPC within 3 business days if significant harm or affects 500+ individuals, mandatory DPO appointment, data portability for business customer data, penalties up to S$1M or 10% of annual Singapore turnover. DNC Registry check required for marketing messages to Singapore numbers.

## PDPA (Thailand, 2019)
**Applies if:** processing personal data of individuals in Thailand (even if controller is outside Thailand).  
**Key requirements:** lawful basis, data subject rights, DPA with processors, breach notification within 72 hours, DPO for large-scale processing.

## APPI (Japan, amended 2022)
**Applies if:** handling personal information of individuals in Japan.  
**Key requirements:** purpose specification, consent for sensitive data, third-party provision restrictions, cross-border transfer restrictions (consent or adequate protection), breach notification to PPC and individuals, local representative required for foreign businesses handling >10,000 Japanese individuals' data.

## PIPL (China, 2021)
**Applies if:** processing personal information of individuals in China.  
**Highest compliance burden:** separate explicit consent per purpose (bundled consent not permitted), data localization for critical infrastructure operators and large-scale processors, security assessment by CAC before cross-border transfers (mandatory if >1M individuals' data or >100,000 sensitive), local representative or entity required, CAC registration for large processors.  
⚠️ Engage a qualified China lawyer — do not attempt compliance without specialist advice.

## POPIA (South Africa, 2020)
**Applies if:** processing personal information of South African residents, or processing occurring in South Africa.  
**Key requirements:** lawful basis, data subject rights, DPA with operators (processors), breach notification to Information Regulator and individuals, mandatory Information Officer, prior authorization for certain processing.

## DPDP Act (India, 2023)
**Applies if:** processing digital personal data of individuals in India.  
**Key requirements:** consent-based processing (or legitimate use), plain-language notice, data principals' rights (access, correction, erasure, grievance), Data Fiduciary obligations, significant Data Fiduciaries must appoint DPO in India, cross-border transfers restricted to government-whitelisted countries, breach notification to Data Protection Board and individuals.

## Australia Privacy Act 1988 (Australian Privacy Principles)
**Applies if:** processing personal information of Australians AND organization has >A$3M annual turnover (small businesses exempt unless health sector or trading in personal information).  
**Key requirements:** 13 APPs covering collection, use, disclosure, quality, security, access/correction. Mandatory Notifiable Data Breach (NDB) scheme: notify OAIC and individuals. Privacy Act reform underway — monitor for updates.

## Privacy Act 2020 (New Zealand)
**Applies if:** processing personal information of New Zealand individuals.  
**Key requirements:** 13 Information Privacy Principles, mandatory breach notification to Privacy Commissioner and individuals if likely to cause serious harm, compliance notices.

## PIPA (South Korea, amended 2023)
**Applies if:** processing personal information of South Korean residents (extraterritorial reach).  
**Key requirements:** strict consent, mandatory Privacy Officer, cross-border transfer restrictions (consent or contractual safeguards), data localization for some sectors, breach notification within 72 hours, fines up to 3% of total sales.

## PDP Law (Indonesia, 2022)
**Applies if:** processing personal data of individuals in Indonesia.  
Four-year grace period — fully in force October 2026.  
**Key requirements:** consent-based processing, data subject rights, DPO, breach notification within 14 days to BSSN, data localization for strategic sectors.

## PDPA (Malaysia, 2010)
**Applies if:** processing personal data in Malaysia for commercial transactions.  
**Key requirements:** consent, notice, disclosure limitation, security, retention limitation, data integrity, access rights. Currently limited to commercial transactions within Malaysia; reform underway.

## Data Privacy Act (Philippines, 2012)
**Applies if:** processing personal information of Philippine citizens or residents (applies outside Philippines if using equipment in Philippines or targeting residents).  
**Key requirements:** lawful basis, data subject rights, DPO appointment, breach notification to NPC within 72 hours, registration of data processing systems with National Privacy Commission (NPC).

## Decree 13/2023 (Vietnam)
**Applies if:** processing personal data of individuals in Vietnam.  
**Key requirements:** consent for processing, data subject rights, cross-border transfer restrictions (government approval or specified conditions), breach notification within 72 hours to Ministry of Public Security.

## UAE Federal PDPL (2021, effective 2023)
**Applies if:** processing personal data of individuals in the UAE (except ADGM and DIFC, which have separate regimes).  
**Key requirements:** consent or legitimate basis, data subject rights, data localization for certain sensitive data, cross-border transfer restrictions, breach notification to UAE Data Office.

## Saudi Arabia PDPL (2021, fully effective 2024)
**Applies if:** processing personal data of Saudi residents.  
**Key requirements:** purpose limitation, explicit consent for sensitive data, data subject rights, transfer restrictions (adequacy or consent), breach notification to SDAIA within 72 hours, mandatory DPO for large-scale processors.

## Switzerland nDSG / revFADP (in force Sept 2023)
**Applies if:** processing data of Swiss residents or where processing has effects in Switzerland.  
Closely mirrors GDPR. EU SCCs generally accepted for cross-border transfers.  
**Key requirements:** lawful basis, transparency, data subject rights, DPA with processors, breach notification to FDPIC if high risk, DPIA for high-risk processing.

## KVKK (Turkey, 2016)
**Applies if:** processing personal data of Turkish residents (limited extraterritorial scope).  
**Key requirements:** explicit consent or statutory basis, data subject rights, registration with KVKK board (VERBİS system), cross-border transfer approval, breach notification within 72 hours.

## LFPDPPP (Mexico, 2010)
**Applies if:** processing personal data of Mexican residents by private entities.  
**Key requirements:** privacy notice (aviso de privacidad), consent, ARCO rights (access, rectification, cancellation, opposition), security measures, cross-border transfer restrictions, designated privacy contact.

## Law 1581 (Colombia, 2012)
**Applies if:** processing personal data of Colombian residents.  
**Key requirements:** authorization (consent), privacy notice, ARCO rights, registration of databases with SIC if required, cross-border transfers only to adequate-protection countries or with authorization.

## COPPA (USA, children)
**Applies if:** app directed to children under 13 in the USA, or knowingly collecting data from under-13s.  
**Key requirement:** verifiable parental consent before collecting any personal information.

## US State Privacy Laws
Beyond CCPA: Virginia (VCDPA), Colorado (CPA), Connecticut (CTDPA), Texas (TDPSA), Utah (UCPA), and growing list of states. If significant US users across multiple states, treat CCPA as the highest bar and apply nationwide.

## FERPA (USA, education)
**Applies if:** app serves US educational institutions receiving federal funding.
